# MIA Customer Intelligence Operating Model

MIA turns source data into trusted customer insight through a governed pipeline:

1. Intake preserves original files, filenames, source versions, and upload metadata.
2. Standardisation maps fields, dates, routes, language, sentiment, severity, and customer intent.
3. Validation separates visible source fields from inferred fields and surfaces low-confidence records for review.
4. Human review can approve, correct, or reject weak batch inferences before they are reused.
5. Reporting exposes route, journey-stage, and customer-theme views for operational decisions.

Every generated or synthetic dataset must remain clearly labelled and must not be presented as real passenger evidence.
